# Pancakes

## Ingredients
- 2 eggs
- 500 ml milk
- 200 g flour
- 1 tbsp sugar
- Pinch of salt
- Butter for frying

## Instructions
1. Whisk eggs, sugar and salt together.
2. Add milk and flour gradually, whisking until smooth.
3. Let the batter rest for 15 minutes.
4. Fry thin pancakes in a buttered pan, about 1 minute per side.
